`NexusGameWiki` includes vendored third-party components that retain their own upstream licenses and notices.

- Dear ImGui
  - Upstream: [ocornut/imgui](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui)
  - Vendored under `vendor/imgui/`
- nlohmann/json
  - Upstream: [nlohmann/json](https://github.com/nlohmann/json)
  - Vendored under `vendor/nlohmann/`
- Raidcore Nexus API definitions
  - Upstream: [RaidcoreGG/RCGG-lib-nexus-api](https://github.com/RaidcoreGG/RCGG-lib-nexus-api)
